How it works. A YOLO11n detector runs on this device and finds cars, two-wheelers, buses, trucks and people several times a second. Each one is tracked, and its path is projected forward at its current speed and direction. When two paths would overlap within 3 seconds, the pair turns amber (a heads-up). Under 1.5 seconds, while closing faster than about 15 km/h, it turns red. Each one is logged with its pattern: crossing paths, same direction (rear-end or side-swipe), head-on, heading into a stopped vehicle, or a person in the path. Parked and stopped vehicles are tracked and labelled "still", so a vehicle heading into one is warned on. Loop uses similar rules on recorded video.

Limits. This is one ordinary camera with no calibration, so distances and speeds are rough and some warnings will be false. It predicts the next 3 seconds of motion at current speed and direction, not crashes. Works best from a fixed, raised spot looking down at a junction. A moving or shaking camera makes everything look like it moves, so the page pauses warnings until it is held still.

Privacy. The video is processed on this device. Nothing is uploaded or saved.

Laptop with DroidCam: start DroidCam on the phone and the laptop, then pick "DroidCam" in the camera list above.
